Issue #78, slice 2: the Shop screen and the flow between Runs. Headless check.

A batch-mode Editor (no window, master volume 0) played from the boot scene. A probe killed Enemies through
Enemy.TakeHit, killed the character, clicked the screens' buttons through Button.onClick, and read the Shop's texts back
from its TMP labels. Credits were put in the bank through PlayerSave.Bank where a step needed more than a Run earned.
"save.json" is the file under Application.persistentDataPath, read back from disk. It was deleted afterwards.
